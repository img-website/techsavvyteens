Help us close the gap

Your donation funds stipends for our teen instructors and provides devices and internet access for kūpuna who have none.

  • Stipends for teen instructors

    $50 per 3-hour session means our young leaders are paid for real, skilled work — not asked to volunteer their way out of poverty.

  • Devices & internet for kūpuna

    Some kūpuna have no device and no connection at all. We close that gap so telehealth, family and services are within reach.

  • Industry certifications

    At 30 hours of service we cover a teen's IC3, Microsoft, or cybersecurity certification — a credential that outlasts the program.
