The next steps

What happens now

Here's exactly how you'll get your login — no guesswork.

  1. 1

    An admin reviews your request

    A real person at Tech Savvy Teens checks every application personally.

  2. 2

    We create your account

    Once you're approved, we set up your account and generate a secure password for you.

  3. 3

    Your login lands in your inbox

    We email you your login ID and password. If you shared a mobile number, we text them to you as well.

  4. 4

    Sign in to the app

    Open the Tech Savvy app, enter your emailed ID and password, and you're in. Download it below so it's ready.

Not approved this time? You'll get a friendly email explaining why — and you're always welcome to re-apply with the same email.
