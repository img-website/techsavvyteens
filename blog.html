News & media

Teen tech champions in action

Television segments, community stakeholder meetings, and a Mayor's award — here's where our ʻōpio have been showing up.

Featured · Future Innovators Award

Honoring digital equity leaders

October 9, 2024

Mayor Rick Blangiardi declared Digital Equity Innovation Day, celebrating organizations and individuals committed to bridging the digital divide in Hawaiʻi. Among the honorees, Tech Savvy Teens proudly received the Future Innovators Award, recognizing their exceptional efforts in promoting digital inclusion for both youth and kūpuna across the state of Hawaiʻi.

    This Kūpuna Power segment with Brickwood Galuteria highlights how the KEY Project is helping Hawaiʻi seniors get confident with technology in a whole new way. At the table

Our ʻōpio speak for themselves

At the 2025 Hawaiʻi Broadband Hōʻike, co-founder Jaffer Dakroub joined the afternoon general session "From Access to Action: What Will We Build Together?" alongside the Hawaiʻi State Public Library System and Rep. Greggor Ilagan — speaking to what equity in broadband looks like when it's put into practice.

When decisions get made about Hawaiʻi's digital future, the young people doing the work are in the room.
