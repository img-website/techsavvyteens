Teen tech champions in action

TV segments, community meetings, and a Mayor's award — see where our ʻōpio show up.

Featured · Future Innovators Award

Honoring digital equity leaders

October 9, 2024

Mayor Rick Blangiardi declared Digital Equity Innovation Day to honor those bridging Hawaiʻi's digital divide. Tech Savvy Teens received the Future Innovators Award for helping both youth and kūpuna get online.
