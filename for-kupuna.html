Always free · No charge, ever

Tech help from teens with real patience.

Sit down one-on-one with a trained Tech Savvy Teen and work through your real questions on your own device — at your pace, in a safe and patient environment. This is not a class. It's relationship-based learning, grounded in aloha.

See 2026 events Host us at your center

What we can help you with

Bring any device and any question. Nothing is too small.

  • Internet & Email Support

    Getting online, sending and organising email, and finding what you need on the web without the frustration.

  • Health & Wellness Technology

    Telehealth video visits, patient portals, medication reminders and health tracking apps.

  • Smartphone & Tablet Help

    Our most requested topic. Settings, apps, text messages, photos — whatever is giving you trouble.

  • Video Calling Guidance

    See your grandchildren on the mainland. We'll set it up and practise together until it feels easy.

  • Online Ordering Assistance

    Groceries, prescriptions and deliveries — ordered safely, without handing over more than you should.

  • WiFi & Printer Setup

    Our #2 most requested help. Getting your laptop, WiFi and printer talking to each other again.

Online safety & scam awareness

Recognising scams that target older adults, creating strong passwords, protecting personal and financial data — and knowing who to call when something feels wrong.
